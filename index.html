Frequently Asked Questions

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Image processing happens directly in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to our servers.

Is ImageTools free?

Yes. The core image editing tools are free to use.

What image formats are supported?

JPG, JPEG, PNG and WebP are supported.

Can I use it on mobile?

Yes. The interface is fully responsive and works on mobile devices.

Do I need to install software?

No. Everything works directly in your web browser without any installation.
